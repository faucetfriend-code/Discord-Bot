# Your Calls, Tracked Automatically

You keep trading exactly how you do now, on your own account, on your own
exchange. The app just records your real trades so you build a verified track
record and followers can mirror you.

## What you do

1. Chart your trade with the TradingView position tool, like always.
2. Screenshot it.
3. Desktop: click the extension. On the go: paste it in the analyst channel.
4. The app reads your entry, stop, and targets and shows them back. Confirm,
   fix a number if needed, hit Fire.

About ten seconds. That is the whole job.

## Why it is worth it

- Your account, your exchange. Trade where you already trade. Different
  exchanges and tokens per analyst are fine.
- Your real fills are the record, not a screenshot guess. Your track record is
  exact because it is your actual executed trade.
- The more it runs, the more followers track and mirror your calls.
- Your numbers get checked. The app verifies entry, stop, and target against
  the risk/reward before anything fires, so a fat-finger gets caught.

## Your keys stay safe

We only ever use trade-only API keys. They cannot withdraw or move funds. You
set them up once and never touch them again.

That is it. Chart, snap, confirm, fire.
