How to read the numbers

  • Shadow R = return ÷ the strategy's fixed stop (RSI 5%, Oracle 1.5%); a full stop is exactly −1. Gate.io 5m candles, 72h horizon, conservative on intra-candle ambiguity.
  • Paper R = per-trade structural R from the parsed stop. The two R units are never added together.
  • Paired tests compare two entry/exit rules on the same signal row. Comparing decision buckets against each other is confounded by time (June, July and August each ran a different gate set) and is used here only to price what a gate blocked.
  • "Current ruleset" = RSI rows since 2026-07-10 that passed every live gate (enter + pending_confirm); the latter would be immediate entries under today's config.
